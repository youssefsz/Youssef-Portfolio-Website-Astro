Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 8, 2024

Introduction

PostStyleTN ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

We believe in complete transparency. PostStyleTN is designed with privacy at its core—we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

Information We Collect

PostStyleTN is designed to respect your privacy. Here's what you should know:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • No Tracking: We do not track your activities, and we do not use any analytics or tracking services.
  • Local Storage Only: All your text content, styling preferences (font family, font size, text color, alignment, and formatting options), and app settings (theme preference) are stored locally on your device using device storage mechanisms.
  • No Cloud Sync: Your content is never uploaded to any external servers or cloud services.
  • No User Accounts: PostStyleTN does not require you to create an account or sign in.

How We Use Your Information

Since we don't collect personal information, we don't use it. The only data that exists is stored locally on your device:

  • Text Content: The text you create and style is stored locally for your convenience, allowing you to continue editing when you return to the app.
  • Style Preferences: Your formatting preferences (fonts, colors, sizes) are saved locally to maintain your preferred settings.
  • Theme Settings: Your light/dark mode preference is saved locally for a consistent experience.

Third-Party Services

PostStyleTN uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: We use Google Fonts to provide font styling options. Font files are downloaded directly from Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to this service. We recommend reviewing Google's privacy practices at fonts.google.com.
  • Share Functionality: When you share content, the native sharing feature of your operating system is used. The shared content goes directly to the app you select (Messages, Email, Social Media, etc.) and is not transmitted through our servers.

Data Storage and Security

Your data security is our priority:

  • All data is stored locally on your device using secure, industry-standard storage mechanisms provided by the operating system.
  • We do not have access to your locally stored data.
  • Deleting the app will remove all locally stored data.
  • You maintain full control over your data at all times.

Children's Privacy

PostStyleTN does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any personal information from any users, our app is safe for users of all ages.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through other means, please contact us.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is stored locally and accessible only by you.
  • Deletion: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data through your device settings.
  • Portability: Content you create can be shared using the built-in share feature.
  • No Consent Required: Since we don't collect personal data, no data processing consent is needed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app.

Changes are effective immediately upon posting. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
