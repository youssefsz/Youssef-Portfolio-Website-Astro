FAQs

Is AllerSnap a medical device?
No. AllerSnap is not a medical device and should not replace professional medical advice. While our AI strives for accuracy, no detection system is 100% reliable.
Which allergens can it track?
Common allergens like peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, eggs, gluten/wheat, soy, shellfish, fish, sesame — plus your custom allergens.
How does barcode scanning work?
We fetch product data from Open Food Facts (ingredients, allergens, categories, nutrition) and run AI analysis tailored to your allergy profile.
Whats included in Premium?
Unlimited scans, barcode & text scanning, detailed history, nutrition insights, and advanced AI features. Priority support with the annual plan.
