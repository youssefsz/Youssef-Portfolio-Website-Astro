A few quick answers.

Is Invox free?

Invox is free to download, with ads in the free version and optional in-app purchases. Invox Pro includes cloud sync, an ad-free experience and extra customization. Check the app for current pricing.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can create invoices without signing up. Your data is stored locally by default. An account is needed if you choose cloud sync.

Can I send invoices as PDFs?

Yes. Preview your invoice, share it by email or messaging apps, or print it from the app. You can also generate receipts.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Invox is available for iPhone and iPad, with layouts designed for larger screens. It requires iOS or iPadOS 13.0 or later.

Does Invox collect payments?

Invox creates invoices and tracks their payment status. You mark an invoice as paid after receiving payment through your usual method.
