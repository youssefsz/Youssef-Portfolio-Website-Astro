Questions

A few useful answers.

No inflated promises. Just the product boundaries that matter before you put an app next to your bicycle.
Does a VeloCare visual review replace a mechanic?

No. Reports describe visible conditions in the submitted photos. They are not a safety certification and do not replace an in-person inspection by a qualified bicycle mechanic.

Can I check one part of my bike?

Yes. VeloCare supports complete-bike checks using several views and focused checks for a specific area when you need a closer look.

When does ride recording begin?

Only after you tap Start Ride. The app can continue recording while the screen is locked or another app is open, and it keeps a protected local draft until you finish and choose to save.

How do visual reviews connect to maintenance?

A saved review can create care tasks with due dates, notes, reminders, completion history, and a link back to the source report.
