Welcome to Dream House Builder! 🏠

Let's help you create your perfect home with these simple steps:

  • Select tools from the left toolbar to start building
  • Use the Wall Tool (🧱) to create your house structure
  • Add doors (🚪) and windows (🪟) to your walls
  • Furnish your space with furniture like sofas (🛋️) and beds (🛏️)
  • Use the zoom buttons (+/-) to adjust your view
  • Toggle the grid for precise placement
  • Save (💾) your design at any time

Pro tip: Use Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+S to save quickly!
